# Content Studio 1.3.3: Workflow Audit

## Changes

- New Pinterest images use a 1080 x 1920 canvas and 9:16 prompts. Existing verified 1000 x 1500 Pins remain exportable; no saved images were overwritten.
- CSV spacing starts at one hour and remains editable. The account-label input is removed, while existing delivery receipts remain intact.
- Export preparation uses four ordered read-only workers, request-scoped URL verification, and serialized WordPress uploads per site. Export still rechecks image identity and public availability before writing.
- Pin creation pipelines title, prompt, and image work per Pin, with two workers and intermediate saved checkpoints.
- Meta preparation uses two ordered workers. Facebook and Instagram reuse one shared 4:5 asset and one source-fingerprint check per export. Article images are not used as fallback sources.
- Impact links and promotional boxes are removed from saved article content when loaded, saved, or published. Amazon/manual unrelated links are preserved. Live WordPress posts are not rewritten automatically.
- Article generation always enables the editorial pass and complete recipe-card requirements. Checks cover measured ingredients, actionable steps, positive yield, numeric times, contradictory totals, and thermometer guidance for raw poultry. Incomplete drafts are retained for review but cannot be published through the app.

## WordPress Retention

Enabled by default, independently of local-file cleanup. It runs shortly after app startup and daily while the app stays open.

Only newly recorded, app-created finished Pinterest uploads qualify. The rule does not sweep old WordPress media or article images, and Meta crops are excluded.

Deletion requires seven days since upload, exact media ID/URL identity, an unattached media record, and a successful check of REST-exposed content types and local articles for references. Unresolved imports, invalid receipt dates, and future schedules stay protected. Confirmed imports are retained until seven days after the later of their scheduled publication or recorded confirmation.

Missing credentials, incomplete reference scans, denied endpoints, and live Pin export/preparation defer deletion. Import outcomes must be recorded in batch history before pending media can expire.

This is not a server-side cron job: cleanup waits until the app is running. Keeping a media library smaller does not by itself guarantee faster page loading.

## Verification

- Full lint, production build, and 264 automated tests passed.
- Isolated browser checks passed for receipt matching, result persistence, failed-only retries, and desktop/mobile layout.
- The packaged Windows app opened in a separate empty profile with a fully loaded renderer, working preload bridge, no renderer exceptions, no broken visible images, and no horizontal overflow.
- Runtime dependency audit: zero reported vulnerabilities after compatible updates.
- Full dependency audit: zero critical, five high and ten moderate findings remain in development/build dependencies. These include legacy Tailwind glob/parsing dependencies and Electron packaging transitive dependencies. Some have no patched compatible release; forced major upgrades or tool downgrades were not applied.
- Compatible Axios and other dependency patches were installed; the critical shell-quote issue was patched.
- No live articles were published, no real WordPress media was deleted, and no production Pinterest import was performed during this audit.

## Limits

Automated recipe checks are not kitchen testing and do not establish nutritional accuracy, universal food safety, or guaranteed CTR/retention. An editor should verify unusual recipes and claims.

Local CSV and network validation cannot guarantee Pinterest acceptance or account limits. Replicate output URLs are temporary and remain unsuitable for delayed scheduling.

WordPress references outside REST-exposed content/local articles, such as hardcoded theme files or private plugin storage, cannot be exhaustively audited from the desktop app.

The local installer is unsigned and explicitly labeled UNSIGNED TEST. Release signing still needs a configured signing certificate/service.
