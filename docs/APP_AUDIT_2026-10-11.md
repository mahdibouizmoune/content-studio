# Content Studio audit and fixes: 1.3.5

## Confirmed causes and fixes

- Pasted article intake only warned about duplicates and allowed them anyway. Intake now excludes duplicates and topic overlaps against both saved titles and other accepted rows in the batch. Exact source URLs are checked independently; tracking parameters do not bypass the check. Existing saved articles are never deleted.
- Intake is rechecked against current storage and committed in one operation, avoiding stale-screen and partial-batch duplicate additions. Original and generated titles are both compared; repeated words no longer inflate similarity scores.
- Read-only inspection of the user's saved articles and recipe logs confirmed that `Total Time: 1 day` and `Servings: about 8` were falsely rejected. The validator now accepts numeric days/weeks and approximate positive yields. Nested recipe markup and formatted ingredient headings are parsed as HTML.
- Some older completed poultry recipes lack thermometer guidance. Incomplete recipes receive up to two validated repair attempts before publication. Failed repairs preserve the source and report needs-review without creating a WordPress post. No safety checks are bypassed.
- Recipe validation runs before spending on article images and again on final content. Publish-time repairs are saved locally, returned to both publisher screens, and accompanied by matching Recipe structured data. Concurrent user edits prevent a stale repair from replacing the draft.
- Batch draft preparation previously ran outside the per-article error handler. An exception could interrupt the rest of the batch or leave the controls busy. Preparation is now inside the handler, and the batch lock is always released.
- Recipe collections incorrectly required four ingredients, causing valid short recipes to be padded or rejected. They now require the actual measured ingredients, not an arbitrary minimum of four.
- Generated article titles were cut mid-word at 70 characters. They now use the existing whole-word truncation helper.

## Audit coverage

Automated regression coverage includes article generation and storage, duplicate intake, recipe completeness, WordPress delivery and reconciliation, Pinterest image identity/hosting/scheduling/CSV, Meta/GHL source identity and delivery, credential boundaries, trusted IPC, HTML sanitation, media retention, and workspace recovery. Additional browser and packaged-app checks use isolated test data, not live publication.

The shipping-runtime dependency audit reports zero known vulnerabilities. The full development dependency audit still reports 15 findings (5 high, 10 moderate) in build-tool dependencies, including Tailwind 3's globbing chain and electron-builder's proxy logging chain. The suggested fixes include a major Tailwind migration and a builder downgrade; these were not applied blindly to a production update. The installer remains an explicitly unsigned local test build because no signing certificate is configured.

Structural and consistency validation is not proof of kitchen testing or comprehensive food-safety review. Fermentation and other sensitive recipes still require appropriate editorial review. Live provider outages, expired credentials, and unknown remote publication outcomes cannot be guaranteed away; those cases remain explicit rather than being reported as success.
